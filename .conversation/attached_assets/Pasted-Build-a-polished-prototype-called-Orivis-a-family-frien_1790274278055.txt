Build a polished prototype called **Orivis**, a family-friendly community safety application for Santa Clara County.

Orivis helps families coordinate children’s bus journeys, report community safety issues, and access a simulated emergency SOS feature.

This is a prototype demonstration. Use simulated AI, simulated authentication, simulated notifications, simulated police contact, simulated transit data, and sample data. Do not connect to real emergency services yet.

- Yellow: lower concentration or lower-severity concerns

## Simulated SOS Feature

The SOS feature is simulated only. Do not contact real police, emergency services, or dispatch systems.

When the child presses SOS:

1. Display a short countdown.
2. Provide a Cancel SOS button.
3. If the countdown finishes, activate simulated SOS mode.
4. Simulate notifying the parent.
5. Simulate notifying a police contact dashboard.
6. Display the child’s simulated emergency location.
7. Open an AI safety guidance interface.
8. Display guidance in a quiet, whisper-style format.
9. Provide a chat window where the child can type or share information.
10. Simulate transmitting child-provided information to the police contact.

The simulated police notification may include:

- Child’s name
- Family account
- Current simulated location
- Emergency activation time
- Situation description
- Information entered in the AI chat
- Simulated prolonged silence
- Simulated unusual movement

For the prototype, include controls for:

- “Simulate prolonged silence”
- “Simulate unusual movement”
- “Send information to police contact”
- “End simulated SOS”

The AI should provide calm, safety-focused guidance. Make it clear that this is a prototype simulation and not a replacement for real emergency services.

Do not claim that the AI can reliably detect danger, silence, unusual movement, or emergencies in real life. Simulate these conditions for demonstration purposes only.
